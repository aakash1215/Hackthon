Whether the audit evidence is complete, mostly complete, partial, or limited based on assessment coverage.

Compliance Score (88%) is calculated only from assessed controls. This should not be interpreted as full regulatory compliance.

Final compliance should only be claimed after achieving 100% assessment coverage or explicitly documenting the remaining unassessed controls.
